# CoreWeave Hacks — Event Details (Sep 12-13, 2026)

Saved verbatim from the event invite for reference while building/pitching.

## About the event

Come plug in and supercharge at our premiere CoreWeave hackathon! If you've had an idea for an autonomous loop that's been burning away at you, this heavily-resourced hack is for you to make it happen. Experience everything you've come to love about Weights & Biases WeaveHacks — this time with the full CoreWeave stack (Weave, ARIA, marimo, and much more).

Join us at our SF office (this is an in-person event) to have the most fun building loops you'll have this year. This round is all about the loop: building agents that cycle through reasoning and action, catching their own mistakes, and pushing towards more powerful results at each pass.

Agent capabilities have advanced enormously over the last year — even by their own doing: better memory systems, new forms of RL and fine-tuning, dynamic tool creation, and self-improving loops. This time we **observe, evaluate, track, document, and self-improve** agent iterations while we play with a robot dog and humanoid!

## Hackers get Fully Connected

Attendees have the chance to attend the Fully Connected conference September 29-October 1 (each ticket ~$1k in value), where a final CoreWeave Hacks award will be presented for the "Most Production-Ready" submission.

Criteria for any non-winning attendee to get a ticket will be announced at the event. Once building starts, find out more from a CoreWeave representative.

**The Best Loop Design winners on 9/13 get tickets and stage time at Fully Connected.**

The winning team gets a stage demo on the final day, presenting their project in front of AI leadership. Build like it matters; the work can carry straight from the hackathon floor onto that stage!

**You will have a second chance to win 2 weeks after CoreWeave Hacks, at our Fully Connected conference!**
